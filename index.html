Our Services

LED TV Repair

Backlight issues, panel replacement, no display, flickering screen, and power supply board repair for all LED TV brands.

LCD TV Repair

Dead pixels, screen bleeding, inverter board failure, colour distortion, and motherboard repair for LCD televisions.

Smart TV Repair

Wi-Fi connectivity issues, software updates, app crashes, motherboard faults, and display problems for all Smart TVs.

OLED TV Repair

Burn-in issues, panel degradation, colour accuracy problems, power board failures, and T-CON board repair for OLED TVs.

Plasma TV Repair

Overheating issues, buzzing sounds, sustain board failures, colour fading, and buffer board repair for Plasma TVs.

CRT TV Repair

Picture tube issues, flyback transformer repair, degaussing problems, vertical & horizontal line fixes for CRT TVs.
